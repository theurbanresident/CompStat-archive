# Source, provenance, and rights

The source documents are published by the City of Wilmington, Delaware, on
the Wilmington Police Department's public website. This repository is an
independent research archive and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the
City or Police Department.

The MIT license applies to the software in this repository. It does not assert
ownership of City source documents, logos, or report design. Every archived
source record retains its original URL, retrieval time, SHA-256 digest, and a
link to its immutable release asset. Contact the repository maintainer with
rights or correction concerns.

CompStat figures are described by the source as preliminary and subject to
change. The source also states that its categories reflect Delaware criminal
codes and differ from FBI Uniform Crime Reporting categories. Researchers
should cite exact report revisions and should not treat this archive as an
official final crime-statistics release.
